The bay

A slow harbour, a fast sea, and nothing in between.

Teluk Senja sits just outside Labuan Bajo, close enough for the harbour, far enough for silence. Every suite faces west across the water, where the islands of Komodo turn violet at dusk.

  • 14sea-facing suites
  • Westfacing, for every sunset

Harbour mornings

Sunset deck

The hour we were named for

Senja means dusk. Every evening the deck fills quietly for grilled reef fish, Flores coffee and the slow drop of the sun behind the islands.

Getting here

Ten minutes from the runway

Fly to Komodo Airport (LBJ) in Labuan Bajo, with direct flights from Bali and Jakarta. We meet every guest at arrivals.
